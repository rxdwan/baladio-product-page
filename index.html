About

01

The Story

Before Baladio, I used to sort my music in folders and treated them as playlists. I listened to my music by opening and playing them in VLC player. But as the number of my songs grew, it became a hassle to organize songs and listen to them. Then I had the idea of building a music player web app. Then the work began. What first started as a weekend project, named "lofi music player" has now, a product loading page, a logo and a proper branding.

02

The Philosophy

Anti-subscription, your music belongs to you.

No monthly payments. No corporate middlemen deciding what belongs in your library. Just your collection, exactly how you arranged it, playing the way you like it.

Music is deeply personal. The songs you keep are a map of who you are. That map should belong entirely to you.
